Identity verification that held up on 500 faces

VerifyMe matches a face against an identity document using MediaPipe landmarks and DeepFace embeddings, with liveness checks to stop someone holding up a photograph. I presented the results at an IEEE international conference at IIT Indore.

A LangGraph agent ranks a hundred candidates against a brief, then holds a conversation about the shortlist and re-ranks when the criteria change. It runs a three-round screen and ends on a hire or no-hire call it can explain, passage by passage.
